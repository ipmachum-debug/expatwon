  - question: 'Can I register a business on any visa?'
    answer: 'No. Your visa must permit business activity. F-series residency visas generally can; most E-series work visas cannot run a side business without permission. D-8/D-9 exist specifically for investors and traders.'
  - question: 'Sole proprietor or corporation — which should I start with?'
    answer: 'Sole proprietorship (개인사업자) is faster and cheaper to open and close; a corporation (법인) separates liability, looks better to partners and banks, and can support a D-8 visa, but adds accounting and registration costs.'
