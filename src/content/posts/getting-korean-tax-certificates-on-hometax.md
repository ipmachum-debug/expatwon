Your bank asks for proof of income. You open Hometax, find a tax certificate,
submit it, and the bank rejects it.

The document is genuine. The National Tax Service issued it. Your name is
right. It proves you have no outstanding national tax arrears.

It does not prove what you earned.

This happens constantly, because several Korean tax documents carry almost the
same English name. In Korean the distinctions are obvious:

| Document | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| **소득금액증명** | Income amounts recorded for the tax period |
| **납세증명서** | No disqualifying national tax arrears, at issuance |
| **납부내역증명** | National taxes the taxpayer paid directly |
| **원천징수영수증** | Income and tax withheld or settled by a payer |
| **사실증명(신고사실없음)** | No relevant filing recorded, for the stated scope |
| **사업자등록증명** | Registered business information |

**Before opening Hometax, establish whether the request is about income, tax
payment, tax arrears, business registration or the absence of a filing.** That
one distinction prevents a rejected application.

ℹ️ Which of these a *lender* will actually treat as income — and which prove
status without proving an amount you can borrow against — is a separate
question, answered in [proof of income in
Korea](/loans/proof-of-income-korea-foreigners/). This guide is about what the
documents are and how to get the right one.

## Start from the question, not the English phrase

| What the institution wants to know | Where to look first |
|---|---|
| How much income did you report? | 소득금액증명 |
| Do you have national tax arrears? | 납세증명서 |
| Which national taxes did you pay? | 납부내역증명 |
| What did your employer withhold? | 근로소득 원천징수영수증 |
| Is there a filing on record? | 사실증명(신고사실없음) |
| Is the business registered? | 사업자등록증명 |
| What VAT sales did it report? | 부가가치세과세표준증명 |

That is each document's primary function, not a guarantee of acceptance.
Institutions add requirements, immigration differs by status, and a business
transaction may need both national and local certificates.

🔴 **Ask for the exact Korean name.** English descriptions are for
conversation; the Korean name is what you select in the government system.

## 소득금액증명 — certificate of income amount

Issued by the NTS from the tax information held for the period. Government24
describes it as a certificate for individuals with comprehensive income,
year-end-settled income or daily employment income who need to establish the
amount of income subject to the relevant taxation records.

This is not a statement of bank deposits. It reflects income processed through
the tax administration system, and it is useful for employees, freelancers and
sole proprietors alike — though the figures and the available certificate type
depend on the income source and the filing information.

**It looks backward at a completed tax period.** Someone applying in 2026 may
be asked for 2025, which tells a lender nothing about their salary in November
2026 if they changed jobs in October. That is why payslips and an employment
certificate often come with it. It is not a real-time salary certificate.

## 🔴 The issuance date, not the filing date

File a comprehensive income-tax return in May, request the certificate the
next morning, and the year is not there. Nothing failed.

| Certificate | Generally available from |
|---|---|
| Income amount — comprehensive income-tax filers | **July** of the following year |
| Income amount — other relevant income recipients | **May** of the following year |
| No-filing factual certificate | **July** of the following year |

So for 2025 income: an employee whose employment income went through the
ordinary year-end settlement is generally from May 2026; a sole proprietor who
filed the 2025 return in May 2026 is generally from July 2026.

⚠️ And a **late-filed** return can need the tax office's review and
determination rather than the regular timetable. **The date you filed does not
set the date the certificate appears.**

If you need proof before July, ask the receiving institution whether it accepts
interim evidence — the filed return, the filing confirmation, business-income
withholding records. Those are not automatic substitutes; the institution
decides. **Do not change the income year or invent entries to make a
certificate appear.**

## Getting it

Hometax, under the certificate issuance functions:

```
증명·등록·신청 → 즉시발급 증명 → 소득금액증명
```

Labels move. If the path is gone, search Hometax for `소득금액증명`.

Sign in at [Hometax](https://www.hometax.go.kr/) with a supported
identity-verification method — foreign residents should make sure the
identification used matches the tax records. Select the certificate, checking
you have the income amount one and not clearance or payment history. **Choose
the income category and tax year**: if the institution asked for 2025, select
2025. Enter the application details the institution needs. Pick the language
and review the disclosure settings — do not reveal more identifying
information than required. Then apply, retrieve it, and *read the issued
document before sending it*.

**Picking the wrong tax year does as much damage as picking the wrong
certificate.**

### Government24 is a second route

The official page is
[Government24's income amount certificate service](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=12100000021),
with online, in-person, mobile, civil-service mail and unmanned-machine
channels, and **no government issuance fee**.

In-person applications by an authorised representative are permitted under the
prescribed conditions; **the online application is not available through a
representative**. Government24 names a valid foreign registration card or
passport among the identification accepted in person, and where an English
certificate is requested the passport information may matter to the
verification.

⚠️ An English information page does not mean every certificate, form and
authentication step is available in English. Confirm the actual service and
the issued language.

## 납세증명서 — national tax clearance

Also called 국세완납증명서 in everyday speech; the NTS confirms the older
expression refers to the same certificate.

Government24 defines it as establishing that, **as of issuance**, the taxpayer
has no outstanding national tax arrears other than amounts subject to specified
extensions, deferrals or similar statutory treatment. That is more precise than
"has paid every tax".

It does not prove income, does not show annual salary, and is not a payment
history.

So a sole proprietor with ₩50 million of business income and no disqualifying
arrears gets a certificate establishing the arrears position — and nothing
about the ₩50 million. **Tax clearance is not income verification.**

### It also does not mean you never owed tax

You can hold a clearance certificate having paid substantial income tax, VAT
and other taxes over the years. The certificate is not saying no liability ever
existed, and a lawful deferral can sit inside the determination — Government24's
definition expressly excludes certain deferred amounts from the ordinary
disqualifying-arrears test.

It is evidence of a specific legal condition, not a ledger.

### Getting it

```
증명·등록·신청 → 즉시발급 증명 → 납세증명서
```

Or [Government24's clearance service](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=12100000011),
with no issuance fee, ordinarily processed immediately for the general-purpose
certificate.

🔴 **Overseas emigration is a different request** with its own procedure and
processing time — Government24 distinguishes it from the general-purpose
certificate, so do not default to the general one for a departure or
international process without checking.

⚠️ Unlike an income certificate covering a closed year, this one speaks to your
status **at issuance**. Check the validity printed on it; a document issued
months ago may not be accepted for a new application.

## National and local clearance are two documents

| Certificate | System |
|---|---|
| 국세 납세증명서 | National tax |
| **지방세 납세증명서** | **Local tax** |

A national certificate does not prove the local position, and the reverse. The
local one is its own
[Government24 service](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=13100000056),
also free.

**An institution asking for "national and local tax clearance certificates" is
not asking for two copies.** For business transactions and some lending
procedures, both get requested — which matters most to foreign business owners.

## 납부내역증명 — what you actually paid

The English for this one collides badly with clearance, which is exactly why
the Korean name matters.

Government24 describes it as showing national taxes **paid directly by the
taxpayer**, identifying the tax category, payment date and amount.

| Payment | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Comprehensive income tax | ₩2,000,000 |
| VAT | ₩1,500,000 |
| Other national tax | ₩500,000 |
| **Total** | **₩4,000,000** |

Hypothetical figures — and the same taxpayer could still have an outstanding
liability nowhere in that list.

**납부내역증명 answers what you paid. It does not answer what you still owe.**

### Salary withholding may not appear here

Your employer withholds monthly and remits under its payroll obligations.
Should you request 납부내역증명 to document it?

Probably not. The certificate concerns national taxes **directly paid by the
taxpayer**, while employment income tax collected and remitted by an employer
sits with the employer's withholding obligations. The evidence you want is the
근로소득 원천징수영수증, which records the employment income and the
withholding and settlement.

So if an institution asks for evidence of employment income and withholding,
the withholding receipt is usually closer than a certificate of taxes you paid
personally. Confirm with the institution either way.

### Getting it

Search Hometax for `납부내역증명`, or use
[Government24's service](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=12100000018)
— online, in-person, mobile and other authorised channels, no fee, service
information updated August 2026. Specify the period, then check the issued
certificate contains what was asked for.

⚠️ If a recent payment is missing, confirm it has been processed and recorded.
A bank transfer confirmation shows a payment was initiated; the official
history depends on the authority's recorded receipt. **A payment made moments
ago is not necessarily in every certificate service yet.**

## 원천징수영수증 — the payer's receipt

This one comes from the income payer, not the government: **근로소득
원천징수영수증** for employment income, **사업소득 원천징수영수증** for
qualifying business income. The withholding agent issues it, and the related
payment information also goes to the NTS.

An employment receipt can carry annual employment income, deductions,
calculated tax, tax withheld and the settlement — related to a government
income certificate, but a different function. How to read the form, including
the 2026 revision, is [your Korean withholding tax receipt,
explained](/business/korean-withholding-tax-receipt-explained/); the annual
settlement behind it is [year-end settlement for foreign
employees](/business/year-end-settlement-for-foreign-employees/).

The question here is narrower: **when the payer's receipt is the right
document, and when a government certificate is required instead.**

## 사실증명(신고사실없음) — no filing on record

Someone may have no comprehensive income-tax return recorded for a period —
because there was no taxable income, because no filing was required, or
because a required return was omitted. **Those are legally different
situations, and the certificate does not distinguish them.**

So it is not proof that the person earned nothing, not a tax exemption
certificate, and not evidence that receipts were non-taxable. It establishes
the absence of relevant filing records for the stated scope, nothing more.

An institution may specifically need that. Someone without an income
certificate may be directed to it. But the requirement has to come from the
institution — **do not substitute it for an income certificate on your own
initiative.**

⏳ And it follows the July timetable: a certificate about the absence of a 2025
filing is generally associated with July 2026. An unfinished income year cannot
have its filing position certified for the full year.

📍 **Channel matters here.** Government24 states that Hometax supports all 13
standardised factual certificate types, while Government24 itself supports only
certain categories including the no-filing record and business-registration
status. **Start at Hometax for a specialised factual certificate** — the
[Government24 factual certificate service](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=12100000352)
is free but narrower.

## 사업자등록증명 — and the one it is confused with

Government24 describes it as a certificate for a person or business registered
with the tax authority needing to establish the registered business
information, with an English form available and no fee. It proves registration
— not profitability. A business with no sales still has a registration record.

🔴 **사업자등록증 is not 사업자등록증명.** The first is the registration
document issued in connection with registering; the second is a certificate
evidencing the registered information. Some institutions specifically want a
recently issued 사업자등록증명 rather than a copy of the original. Related
content, not always interchangeable — so ask which Korean document is wanted.
Registration itself, and why it is not immigration permission, is [registering
a business in Korea as a
foreigner](/business/registering-a-business-in-korea-as-a-foreigner/).

## 부가가치세과세표준증명 — VAT sales

Certification of a registered business's VAT filing information — useful for
showing the scale of taxable activity, and **not** an income certificate.

```
VAT taxable sales excluding VAT   ₩120,000,000
business expenses                  ₩95,000,000
illustrative business income       ₩25,000,000
```

One certificate concerns reported VAT taxable sales; the other concerns income
recorded through the income-tax system. An institution may want both, because
they establish different facts.

ℹ️ There is a separate certificate for qualifying VAT-exempt business revenue —
**부가가치세면세사업자수입금액증명**. A VAT-exempt business should not assume
the taxable-sales certificate is the right evidence of its revenue.

## The bundled application

Government24's **국세 제증명 일괄 신청** covers six national tax certificates
in one request: clearance, payment history, VAT taxable sales, business
registration, business suspension and business closure. Online, applicant
personally, no fee.

🔴 **The income amount certificate is not in that six.** Check the listed
documents before submitting rather than assuming the bundle covers everything a
bank asked for.

## What it costs

The Government24 pages for the principal certificates here — income amount,
clearance, payment history, business registration, and the factual certificate
service — state **no government issuance fee**.

That is the issuance service. It is not every cost: a professional may be paid
to help, an overseas institution may require translation, authentication or
courier. And a taxpayer who needs to fix an unfiled or inaccurate return faces
fees and tax consequences that have nothing to do with a certificate fee.

**Do not confuse the cost of issuing a certificate with the cost of fixing the
records behind it.**

## In person, and when sign-in fails

Tax offices and applicable local government service locations issue the
principal certificates, each with its own service rules — check the processing
institution and authorised channels before travelling. Personal applications
accept a foreign registration card, passport or other applicable
government-issued identification, depending on the service. A representative
needs a power of attorney, their own identification and evidence of
authorisation; **online applications generally do not permit representatives.**

⚠️ An unmanned certificate machine may use authentication a given foreign
resident cannot complete. A certificate existing at a kiosk does not mean every
foreigner can get it there.

**If Hometax will not let you in**, a valid ID is not the whole story. The name
in the tax records may differ from the one a bank or authentication provider
holds; a certificate may be registered incorrectly; the account may sit on an
older identification record. A failed login does not tell you which.

Check that the information used for authentication matches the official
record, try another supported method, then contact the NTS — **126** — or visit
an authorised office with identification. 🔴 **Do not create inconsistent
taxpayer identities to get a certificate, and do not use someone else's
credentials to obtain your own records.**

## When the certificate is wrong

A certificate reflects what the authority holds. If the underlying information
is wrong, reissuing the same certificate changes nothing.

An employee whose employer reported income incorrectly should review the
withholding receipt and the employer's payment statement; where there was a
reporting error, the payer or taxpayer uses the correction process. For a sole
proprietor the problem may be in the return itself — an omitted sale, a wrong
expense calculation, an amendment.

🔴 **Do not edit the figures on a government-issued PDF.** A certificate has to
remain an authentic record of the issuing authority.

**If the tax data is wrong, correct the tax data. Reprinting does not correct
the record.**

### Or missing, because nothing was reported

A freelancer paid by Korean clients with 3.3% deducted checks Hometax and
cannot find the income. Possible reasons: the statements are not yet
processed; the payer used wrong identifying information; the income was
classified differently; the payer never submitted it correctly. A bank
statement showing a net payment settles none of them.

Request the withholding information from the payer and compare it against the
NTS records. If a required return was never filed, that obligation needs
addressing too. **Do not build a certificate around estimated income when the
official records do not exist** — this guide is about getting the right
certificate once the records do.

## Four requests, and the first document each time

**A visa extension asking for 소득금액증명** → the income amount certificate
for the specified year. The employer's withholding receipt may support it; it
is not an automatic substitute.

**A client asking for registration and clearance** → both 사업자등록증명 *and*
납세증명서. Neither replaces the other.

**An institution asking what you paid** → 납부내역증명. The clearance
certificate is not a payment ledger.

**An overseas institution** → identify the Korean document first, then confirm
whether the official English version is accepted or a certified translation is
required. The English titles for payment history and clearance read almost
identically, so the English name alone is not safe identification. And check
the name and identifying details can be matched reliably to the passport — if
they cannot, ask the issuing authority rather than editing anything.

⚠️ **Immigration decides what satisfies an immigration application; the NTS
only issues the document.** There is no single certificate universally required
for every extension — some applications assess income, others employment,
sponsorship, investment or family relationships. For current requirements, use
the official immigration service information or the Immigration Contact Center
on **1345**.

## Before you submit

Check five things: the **Korean document name** matches what was requested; the
**identity information** corresponds to the applicant; the **period** covers
the year or payment range asked for; the **issue date and validity** are recent
enough; and the **disclosure settings and language** meet the institution's
requirement.

The institution may also want a specific submission format — the electronically
issued original, a verifiable PDF, a printed copy. Confirm that before sending
sensitive tax information.

**A correct certificate still gets rejected if the wrong year, format or
identity details were selected.**

## The five mistakes

**Submitting clearance as income proof.** No disqualifying arrears is not an
earnings figure.

**Treating a withholding receipt as 소득금액증명.** One comes from the payer,
one from the NTS. Related figures, different documents.

**Requesting the newest year too early.** July for comprehensive filers, May
for other relevant categories.

**Confusing national and local.** Two systems, two certificates.

**Choosing by English name.** "Tax payment certificate" and "tax clearance
certificate" are almost the same words for almost opposite facts.

```
identify the question
  → confirm the Korean name
  → select the period
  → request it
  → verify what was issued
```

Hometax and Government24 both issue these, and for the common ones the
government fee is zero. But picking the wrong document still costs time, and it
costs it against a bank's, an immigration office's or a counterparty's
deadline.

**The most useful Korean tax certificate is not the one with the most
information on it. It is the one that proves exactly what was asked.**
