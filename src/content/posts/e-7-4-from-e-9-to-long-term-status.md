For a worker on E-9, almost every rule points the same way: a fixed employment
activity period, workplace changes only on statutory grounds, no family
accompaniment, and a departure at the end.

E-7-4 skilled technical status is the one structural exit — and it is not a
promotion that arrives with seniority. It is a rationed status with a threshold,
a score and a quota.

## What actually changes

The wage rise is the least of it. Moving from E-9 to E-7-4 replaces one
regulatory system with another:

| | E-9 | E-7-4 |
|---|---|---|
| Framework | Employment Permit System | E-7 residence management |
| Changing workplace | Statutory grounds, counted | New employer meets E-7 conditions |
| Duration | Commonly 4y10m, then departure or re-entry cycle | Up to two years per period, extendable |
| Family | Not available | Accompaniment permitted |
| Onward | Limited | F-2 and F-5 pathways exist |

The Ministry of Justice operates it as a long-stay settlement track for skilled
workers rather than as labour supply — which is a description of intent, and
also of what the status actually does.

## The eligibility threshold

The base group for 2026 is a **registered foreign national with four or more
years of stay on E-9, E-10 or H-2 within the last ten years**.

Then the points: annual income, Korean language ability, age, work history,
employer recommendation among the criteria. And the wage requirement for
E-7-4 is [₩26,000,000 a year](/tracked/e7-annual-wage-requirement/) for 2026 —
the same announcement that sets the other E-7 sub-types.

<div class="callout callout-note">
  <p class="callout-title">Two of the criteria take years to build</p>
  <p>Korean language ability and a documented work history are not assembled in
  the month before an application. Neither is an employer recommendation from a
  company that has known you long enough to give one. Anyone treating this as a
  route should be building those from year one, not deciding to try in year
  four.</p>
</div>

## The quota is the part most guides omit

E-7-4 is capped. For 2026 the annual issuance figure is
[33,000](/tracked/e74-annual-quota/), with the scope of eligible employment
widened in some sectors including agriculture and fisheries.

A quota means qualifying is a threshold rather than an entitlement. Whether the
route is realistically open in a given year is a fact about policy, not about
the applicant — and it has been expanded before, so it is worth checking rather
than assuming in either direction.

## The timing problem nobody mentions

Look at the two numbers together:

```
E-7-4 eligibility          4 years on E-9 / E-10 / H-2
E-9 employment activity    commonly 4 years 10 months
```

Ten months. That is the window in which someone becomes eligible and is still
lawfully working in Korea on the status that made them eligible.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
  <p class="callout-title">Which makes preparation a year-three activity</p>
  <p>Income records, a language qualification, work history and an employer
  recommendation gathered in month 50 of a 58-month period is not a plan. And
  missing a quota year does not pause
  <a href="/tracked/e9-max-continuous-employment-period/">the employment activity
  period</a> — it keeps running.</p>
</div>

The re-entry special case exists for workers who complete the period without
converting: depart, and after a short time outside Korea — currently one month —
return on E-9. That resets a cycle. It does not accumulate toward settlement in
the way E-7-4 does.

## E-7-4 does not become F-2 or F-5 by itself

The pathway is real and it is not automatic.

F-2 residence and F-5 permanent residence each carry their own conditions:
length of stay in Korea, income, conduct, basic civic knowledge, residence
history, an established basis of life here. The requirements differ by which
F-2 or F-5 sub-category is being applied for, and by the E-7 sub-type held.

```
E-9  →  E-7-4  →  F-2 or F-5
```

<div class="callout callout-warn">
  <p class="callout-title">A roadmap, not an escalator</p>
  <p>Each arrow is a separate application assessed on its own criteria at the
  time it is made, against rules that can move between steps. Reaching E-7-4 is
  a substantial change in what you may do in Korea. It is not a place in a
  queue.</p>
</div>
